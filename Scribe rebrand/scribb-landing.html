Entry 003 What Scribb is

Scribb is the Agent of Records.

Most organizations already have the information they need — but it's spread across spreadsheets, emails, folders, finance tools and staff memory. Scribb brings critical records into one intelligent system that helps your organization understand what happened, what is required, and what must be reported.

Record→ Relate→ Review→ Report→ Readiness
Entry 004 How it works

How Scribb works.

01 / RECORD

Record

Capture transactions, documents, decisions, activities and evidence as they happen, while the context is still fresh.

02 / RELATE

Relate

Connect each record to its project, obligation, fund, member or reporting requirement — nothing floats on its own.

03 / REVIEW

Review

Scribb identifies missing information, approaching deadlines, risks and incomplete records before they become problems.

04 / REPORT

Report

Generate the outputs your institution needs for management, boards, donors, audits, taxes and regulators.
